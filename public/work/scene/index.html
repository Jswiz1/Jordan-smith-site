            AboutThe world, then Doncaster

            Ten years travelling. Ninety-odd countries.

            For ten years, work flew me into ports all over the world. More than ninety countries, most of them a few days at a time. The job isn't the interesting part. What I saw along the way is.

            Somewhere in the middle I spent three years in Dubai at Worldwide Golf, learning video, photography, marketing and publishing on the job, then a year in Miami working in the yacht industry.

            Everywhere I went, I watched how things were done. From a tiny local coffee shop to a global organisation, every place did it a little differently, and the best ones had ideas worth stealing.

            I brought all of it back to Doncaster and started building: a production studio, a coffee brand, a local media platform and the product that grew out of it. Largely self-taught, and mostly by doing it in my own businesses first.
